Shipping & returns

Shipping (US only). Every piece is made to order and ships in 5 to 7 days, then takes about 2 to 5 days to arrive. Shipping is calculated at checkout from your delivery address and the weight of your order, using USPS Ground Advantage retail prices, and shown before you pay.

Returns. Standard-color pieces can be returned within 14 days of delivery, unused and in their original packaging. Send me a message first, then mail the piece back. Return shipping is paid by the buyer. Your refund goes back to your PayPal or card within 5 business days of the piece arriving.

Custom colors are made just for you, so they're final sale.

Digital downloads arrive instantly with no shipping. Because they can't be returned, they're final sale, but if a file won't open, email me and I'll fix it or refund you.

Broken or damaged? Send me a photo within 3 days of delivery and I'll make you a new one free, or refund you in full. No need to send it back.

Questions? Use the contact form or email sfvrouqa@gmail.com. Sana Handicrafts, Fort Smith, Arkansas.
